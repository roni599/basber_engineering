About Us

"Providing Electric Service Since 2010"

Since 2010, We have been providing Electric Service to the industrial, commercial, and residential sectors under the banner of Bachar Electric Service. Then we started manufacturing Transformers and Substation Equipment in 2018 from the well engineered manufacturing plant of Bachar Engineering Ltd.

Learn More

Why Choose Us

We are committed to design, manufacturing, and giving timely support to all kinds of Transformers and switchgear with professionalism and dedication to achieve the complete satisfaction of our clients.

Quality is our first Priority

We are a reliable products and service provider, we work with most of the top quality supplier brands for raw materials.

24/7 Service facility

Up to 36 months warranty from the date of delivery of our Transformers. We are ready to provide you service any time of the day.

On time Delivery

we successively meet or overcome our client’s expectations. We always try to fulfill our clients wants and needs.
